Fret Master

Fret Master is a focused fretboard trainer for guitarists of all levels. Drill every note on every fret, learn intervals with real guitar shapes, and track your progress — all offline, no ads, no distractions.

  • Note recognition with real-time pitch detection
  • Intervals quiz with playable guitar shapes
  • String-mode and natural-notes filter
  • Built-in chromatic tuner
  • Optional metronome (40–240 BPM)
  • Works 100% offline — no data collected
